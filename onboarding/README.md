## Fast-Track Onboarding Principles

1. **Learn by Doing** — We emphasize hands-on learning over passive training
2. **AI-First Approach** — Every new joiner is encouraged to leverage AI tools to accelerate learning and productivity
3. **Early Ownership** — New hires are expected to take ownership of small deliverables within the first month
4. **Continuous Feedback** — Managers provide frequent feedback during the first 30 days. Be proactive in talking to your manager, colleagues, and HR for any assistance.

---

## By the End of Month 1, You Should:

- Be confident in your role
- Understand key processes, people, products, and teams
- Deliver small contributions as per your manager
- Be fully ready to operate well from Month 2

## Quick Start Checklist (Week 1)

- [ ] Complete mandatory HR processes and compliance paperwork
- [ ] Set up tools, access, and systems (laptop, email, Jira, Teams, HubSpot, etc.)
- [ ] Attend company induction sessions
- [ ] Meet your manager and team members
- [ ] Learn communication norms and tools
- [ ] Read through this wiki — start with [Company](../company/README.md) and [Products](../products/README.md)
