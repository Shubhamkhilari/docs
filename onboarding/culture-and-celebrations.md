# Culture & Life at Xoxoday

## Our Culture Philosophy

Culture is not a fixed idea — it grows with people, adapts with context, and deepens with experience. At Xoxoday, culture is a living system shaped by what we learn, listen to, and how we respond.

- We ask for feedback regularly and act on it
- We revisit our practices to ensure they're good for people and business
- We treat culture as shared work, not leadership property
- We welcome new voices and let them influence how we work
- We know that what worked once may not work forever
- We may not have a perfect culture — but we aim for an **honest** one

## We Practice What We Preach — Living Empuls

At Xoxoday, Empuls isn't just a product we build — it's a platform we live on every day.

Our teams use Empuls to:
- Recognize contributions and celebrate milestones in real time
- Communicate updates, share wins, and stay connected across teams and geographies
- Access employee benefits, rewards, and wellness initiatives
- Run surveys and gather feedback
- Drive internal incentive programs and team challenges

By using Empuls ourselves, we gain invaluable insights that help us innovate and enhance the product for our customers.

## Company Events

### Annual Anniversary Outing
Once a year, we organize our Annual Anniversary Outing — a chance to disconnect, recharge, and celebrate our journey. Think beautiful scenery, non-stop laughter, and memorable karaoke sessions!

### Team Outings & Dinners
Every team gets its own mini escape with a budget to go have fun. Good food + good company = great teamwork. Ideas spark, laughter flows, and bonds go beyond the work.

### Fun Evenings
Some evenings, we turn off the spreadsheets and turn up the music — jamming, games, and pure joy where guitars, laughter, and friendly banter fill the room.

### Birthday Celebrations
We celebrate birthday **months** — when one teammate has cake, we all get cake. Add balloons, laughter, and some very questionable singing!

### Quarterly Townhalls
- **Leadership Insights:** Key business updates from leadership
- **Recognition:** LSA Awards, Trophies, R&R moments for every team member
- More than just a meeting — our quarterly recharge with applause, high-fives, and shared enthusiasm
