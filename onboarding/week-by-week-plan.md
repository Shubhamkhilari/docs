# Week-by-Week Onboarding Plan

---

## Week 1 — Orientation & Context
*Understand the Big Picture*

- Understand Xoxoday's vision, values, and business model
- Learn the product ecosystem (Empuls, Plum, Loyalife)
- Understand key departments and workflows
- Complete mandatory HR processes, compliance, IT & desk setup
- Set up tools, access, and systems
- Get to know people around you
- Attend company induction sessions
- Meet manager and team members
- Learn communication norms and tools

---

## Week 2 — Role Clarity & Skill Immersion

- Know and acquaint yourself with the tools used in your role
- Understand your role responsibilities
- Learn department processes
- Review customer use cases and success stories
- Shadow team members
- Attend role-specific training
- Understand key KPIs and success metrics
- Observe live customer interactions

---

## Week 3 — Hands-On Execution

- Start handling small tasks independently
- Participate actively in team discussions
- Work on initial assignments
- Attend cross-functional meetings
- Understand decision-making processes

---

## Week 4 — Ownership & Integration

- Take ownership of small projects
- Demonstrate understanding of workflows
- Build stakeholder relationships
- Seek feedback from manager
- Deliver first measurable output
