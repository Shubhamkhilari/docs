# Department-Wise 30-Day Onboarding Focus

---

## Sales

- Understand ICP, products, pricing, and use cases for all three products
- Shadow demos and client calls
- Learn CRM workflows (HubSpot)
- Deliver first pipeline contribution

---

## Customer Care & Customer Success

- Understand onboarding processes
- Learn support workflows and ticketing system
- Attend customer meetings
- Handle small client interactions
- Understand customer ticket categories
- Start handling simpler tickets independently

---

## Marketing

- Learn brand guidelines
- Understand the sales process, lead generation, campaigns, and funnels
- Understand the various functions in marketing and how they work with sales
- Understand marketing budgets, SQL targets, CAC, meetings, pipeline, and revenue goals
- Understand geographical and product-related marketing nuances
- Understand the various channels used to drive meetings for sales
- Contribute to first campaign or content piece

---

## Product Management

- Understand product roadmap for your assigned product
- Understand the tech stack, project management, and how tech & product teams work together
- Learn customer problems through calls and documentation
- Shadow customer calls
- Shadow sprint & scrum calls
- Write your first PRD or feature analysis

---

## Engineering

- Set up development environment
- Understand the product roadmap & the product you're working on
- Understand how tech delivery works and how the tech team works with other stakeholders
- Understand the architecture
- Join scrum/sprint calls
- Deliver first code contribution

---

## Catalog / Vendor Operations

- Learn vendor onboarding workflows
- Understand the various reward categories in the catalog
- Understand regional nuances in categories (India vs USA vs META vs SEA)
- Understand reward fulfillment processes
- Understand the revenue model: margins, expiry, contracts
- Learn about existing vendors and catalog coverage
- Handle first vendor interactions

---

## HR / People

- Learn company policies and engagement processes
- Participate in hiring or employee initiatives
- Understand payroll, performance, and appraisals
- Understand the HRMS, policies, processes, and compliance
- Start talking to employees and shadow your manager

---

## Finance

- Understand billing and revenue workflows
- Learn key financial systems (Jira, accounting tools, MIS)
- Understand the revenue model, AR, AP, MIS
- Start contributing to assigned tasks
