## Our Hiring Philosophy

Hiring is not just about filling roles — it is about building a community of people who share values, demonstrate ownership, and are committed to growing together.

We strive for an **inclusive hiring process** that values:
- Potential over credentials
- Diversity of thought
- Genuine passion to create meaningful impact

---

## Where We Hire From

- Career page and direct applications
- Employee referrals — **30–40% of all new hires** come through referrals
- Campus hiring for internships and full-time roles
- Select recruitment partners for specialized positions

> Employees who refer successful candidates are eligible for referral rewards as per company policy.

---

## What We Look For

1. **Ownership and Accountability** — Takes responsibility, follows through, proactively solves problems
2. **Learning Agility and Adaptability** — Curious, open to feedback, willing to evolve
3. **Collaboration and Teamwork** — Works well with diverse cross-functional teams
4. **Communication Skills** — Clear, structured, professional
5. **Problem-Solving and Analytical Thinking** — Critical thinking, practical solutions
6. **Customer-Centric Mindset** — Understands impact of work on customers
7. **Integrity and Professionalism** — High ethical standards, honesty, respect
8. **Grit and Consistency** — Perseverance, resilience, long-term commitment
9. **AI Mindset & Innovation Orientation** — Proactive use of AI tools to enhance productivity
