# Hiring Process

## Our Hiring Philosophy

At Xoxoday, hiring is a **two-way process**. Just as we evaluate candidates, we encourage candidates to evaluate us — our culture, values, and growth opportunities.

We look for **alignment over résumé**: culture & values fit, attitude over aptitude, and future potential over past credentials. We're always open to passionate individuals from non-traditional backgrounds.

---

## What We Look For

| Attribute | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Culture & Values Alignment | Long-term success comes from shared values and purpose |
| Attitude Over Aptitude | Ownership and work ethic are harder to cultivate than technical skills |
| Future Potential | We focus on ability to grow, adapt, and deliver results — not just past experience |
| Ownership & Accountability | Takes responsibility, proactively solves problems, follows through |
| Learning Agility | Curious, open to feedback, willing to evolve in a fast-growing org |
| Collaboration | Works well with cross-functional and global teams |
| Communication | Clear, structured, professional — essential for most roles |
| Problem-Solving | Critical thinking and practical, logical approaches to challenges |
| Customer-Centric Mindset | Understands how their work impacts customers |
| Integrity | High ethical standards, honesty, respect in all interactions |
| Grit & Consistency | Perseverance, resilience, long-term commitment |
| AI Mindset | Proactive use of AI tools to enhance productivity and innovate |

## Hiring Process Stages

### 1. Application & Initial Screening
Candidates apply via careers page, referrals, campus hiring, or recruitment partners. Resume screened for role alignment and cultural fit potential.

### 2. Recruiter Interaction
Initial discussion to understand:
- Role expectations
- Candidate experience and motivations
- Cultural alignment
- Compensation expectations

Candidates can also ask questions about the company and role.

### 3. Functional / Technical Assessment
Depending on the role:
- Technical interviews
- Case discussions
- Role-specific assignments
- Problem-solving exercises

Focus: Skills, knowledge, and practical application.

### 4. Hiring Manager Interaction
Discussion on:
- Role responsibilities and expectations
- Success metrics
- Working style and team dynamics

### 5. Cross-Functional / Cultural Interviews
Interactions with other stakeholders to evaluate collaboration, communication, and cultural alignment.

### 6. Leadership Interaction (Senior Roles)
For leadership or critical roles: strategic thinking, long-term potential, and alignment with organizational vision.

### 7. Reference Checks
Where applicable — validate experience and professional conduct.

### 8. Offer & Onboarding
Offer extended with role, compensation, and joining timeline details. Onboarding process helps new hires integrate smoothly.

---

## Making an Informed Decision

We encourage candidates to:
- Reflect on why they want to join Xoxoday
- Understand responsibilities, expectations, and growth opportunities
- Interact with potential managers and team members
- Speak with current employees about work culture
- Evaluate the opportunity holistically: learning, career growth, team quality, vision, and compensation

---

## A Note on Response Times

Due to the high volume of applications, we may not always respond individually to every candidate. Candidates are welcome to follow up politely if they believe their application may have been missed.
