# Internships & Freelancers

## Internships at Xoxoday

Internships are an opportunity for **mutual learning and growth**. We welcome interns who are curious, willing to take initiative, and passionate about building meaningful solutions.

### Guidelines

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Minimum duration | 2 months |
| Preferred duration | **6 months** — ensures meaningful learning and contribution |
| Applications | Via the careers section of our website |
| Eligibility | Students across colleges, locations, and academic backgrounds |
| Projects | Real projects with real responsibilities — hands-on learning |
| Stipend | Varies based on role, duration, and performance |
| Benefits | Eligible for certain company benefits; insurance, loans, ESOPs, and performance bonuses are reserved for full-time employees |
| Full-time conversion | High-performing interns may be considered based on performance, business needs, and role availability |

## Freelancers & Consultants

Xoxoday collaborates with freelancers, gig professionals, and consultants to complement internal capabilities and support specialized projects.

### When We Engage External Professionals
- Specialized expertise not available internally
- Additional bandwidth for time-bound initiatives

### Functions That Frequently Work with Freelancers
- Marketing, design, and content creation
- Finance, legal, and compliance
- Technology and product development
- Sales & business development

### Engagement Structure
- Can be individuals or through consulting organizations
- Scope, duration, and compensation determined by respective teams

### Full-Time Conversion
Freelancers/consultants who demonstrate strong alignment with values and consistent performance may be considered for full-time roles, subject to mutual interest and business requirements.

### How to Engage
Explore opportunities via the careers page or the official careers contact channels.
