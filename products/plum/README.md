# Plum — Reward Distribution & Redemption Platform

## What is Plum?

Plum is the reward infrastructure layer that sits between the **demand** for rewards (use cases) and **supply** (reward options). It enables businesses to distribute, manage, and fulfill rewards across 100+ countries via multiple channels and formats.

## How Plum Works

### Two Adoption Models

1. **Portal/Admin-led Rewards Distribution**
   Business teams run programs and distribute rewards through the Plum Admin Portal — sent as codes, links, points, or direct gift cards via email/WhatsApp/SMS.

2. **Rewards Store Frontend (API/SSO)**
   Platforms fulfill rewards using REST APIs, SSO-based Plum storefront, or a custom storefront. End users redeem at a URL like `stores.xoxoday.com`.

## Reward Distribution Formats

| Format | Best For |
|---|---|
| **Reward Points (Xoxo Points)** | Repeat rewards to same user, secure, login required |
| **Reward Codes (Xoxo Codes)** | Guest checkout, flexible, physical or digital, transferable |
| **Reward Links (Xoxo Links)** | Unique per-recipient link, simple one-click redemption |
| **Direct Gift Cards** | Specific brand vouchers, no Xoxoday catalog involved |
