# Products

Xoxoday has three products that span the full spectrum of rewards, engagement, and loyalty.

---

## Product Suite

| Product | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| [Empuls](./empuls/README.md) | AI-powered employee engagement platform | HR, People teams, Sales leaders — 200+ employee companies |
| [Plum](./plum/README.md) | Reward distribution & redemption platform | Any company needing reward infrastructure — 10 to 100,000 employees |
| [Loyalife](./loyalife/README.md) | Enterprise loyalty platform | Consumer & channel loyalty — mid to enterprise, 1,000+ employees |

## Reward Program Types

| Type | Description | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| **Closed loop** | Rewards earned and burned within specific brand/company. E.g. airlines, retail | Better data insights, brand stickiness, generally no GMV revenue |
| **Open loop** | Burns anywhere in the world — maximum flexibility | Survey rewards, banking loyalty, fintech — lower GMV revenue |
| **Semi-closed** | Earn from one place, burn at select places | Coalition programs, channel incentives — better GMV margins |
