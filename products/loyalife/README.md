# Loyalife — Enterprise Loyalty Platform

## What is Loyalife?

Loyalife is an enterprise-grade loyalty management platform acquired via Giift in 2022. It enables businesses to build and run complex consumer loyalty, channel loyalty, influencer loyalty, and coalition loyalty programs — both on cloud and on-premise.

## Types of Loyalty Programs

| Type | Description | Loyalife Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Consumer Loyalty (B2C)** | Repeat purchase, CLTV, brand engagement — points per purchase, cashback, tier-based benefits | Strong in Banking/Fintech; Medium in E-commerce/Telecom |
| **Channel Loyalty (B2B)** | Push products through distribution channels — invoiced-based rewards, tier programs for dealers | Strong with Distributors, Dealers, Wholesalers in FMCG, FMCD, Automotive |
| **Influencer Loyalty** | Engage influencers, creators, brand advocates, tradespeople | Strong — referral rewards, campaign rewards, gamification |
| **Coalition Loyalty** | Multiple brands in one loyalty ecosystem | Supported |

## Key Differentiators

1. **On-Premise & Cloud Deployments** — Data sovereignty, critical for banking/finance compliance
2. **Full-Stack Complex Loyalty Solution** — Multi-layered rules, intricate programs beyond simple point accrual
3. **Truly Global** — 24/7 support, 100+ languages, 50+ currencies, multi-region deployment
4. **Vast Catalog** — 10M+ reward options in 175+ countries via Plum marketplace
5. **Agentic AI** — In accrual, redemption, consumer behavior patterns
6. **Highly Configurable** — Self-serve platform with advanced marketing tools, segmentation, analytics

## ICP

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Min. company size | 1,000+ employees |
| Loyalty rewards budget | $1M+ annually (platform fee is ~1/100th of rewards budget) |
| Deployment | Understands why they need loyalty; has clear requirements and launch dates |
| Champion | Clear loyalty program champion with defined KPIs |

### Disqualifiers
- Company < 500 employees
- Not clear on why they need a loyalty solution
- Loyalty budget for rewards < $0.5M USD
- Purchase frequency in industry > 6 months
- No defined champion
