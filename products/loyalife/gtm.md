# Loyalife — GTM, Sales & ICP

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Company size | Mid to enterprise, 1,000+ employees |
| Loyalty rewards budget | $1M+ annually |
| Program clarity | Clear objectives, champion, and launch timeline |
| Technical readiness | API-capable, DMS/CRM/CDP + POS/ecom in place |

---

## Economic Buyers

Customer experience leaders, CMO / Chief Customer Officer / Chief Commercial Officer, sometimes Head/VP Loyalty & CRM.
For channel programs: VP Sales / VP Channel / CRO.

## Champion
Loyalty Program Owner / CRM Lead / Retention or Growth Lead / Head of Digital Marketing — the person who owns KPIs and runs the program day-to-day.

## Key Evaluators
Enterprise Architect / Integration Lead, CISO / InfoSec, Data/Analytics Lead (CDP/CRM owner), Finance (business case), Procurement, Legal/Compliance, Ops/Customer Support.

## Influencers / Blockers
- **CIO/CTO:** Blocks if integration effort looks heavy or stack conflicts
- **CISO / DPO:** Blocks on data residency, encryption, access control, audits
- **Procurement:** Blocks on pricing, contract terms, vendor risk
- **Finance Controller:** Blocks if ROI model is weak or costs seem "nice-to-have"
- **Incumbent vendor / SI partner:** Blocks to protect existing revenue

---

1. Security / compliance

6. Global support
7. Pricing
