# Empuls — Features & Modules

Clients can choose one or more modules as per their requirements (modular pricing).

---

## 1. Rewards & Recognition (R&R)

Build a culture of appreciation through top-down and peer-to-peer recognition.

- **Award types:** Spot, Approval, Jury-based workflows; democratized awards for self-nominations
- **Milestone programs:** Automate recognition for service anniversaries and key moments
- **Core value badges:** Tie recognition to organizational values
- **COLI (Cost of Living Index):** Adjust rewards equitably for a global workforce
- **Budget management:** Advanced governance rules, reward abuse reports (quid pro quo), structured notification campaigns
- **Leaderboards:** Gamified engagement, particularly for blue-collar workforce
- **Customizable challenges:** Across sales, learning, wellness, productivity, team building, CSR

---

## 2. Sales Incentives & Commissions

Automate and disburse sales incentives & commissions at scale.

- **Incentive Program Design:** Custom plan builder with multiple structures (territory, quota, roles, SPIFFs, contests, commissions)
- **Commission Management:** Plan management, calculations, payouts, statements
- **Automated calculations:** Multi-layer incentive rule engine, real-time tracking, error-free payouts
- **Sales Performance Tracking:** Individual dashboards, progress toward milestones
- **Gamification:** Sales contests, leaderboards, badges, rankings
- **SPIFFs & Campaign Management:** Product launch incentives, seasonal campaigns
- **CRM Integrations:** Salesforce, HubSpot — automated incentive calculations from deal data
- **Analytics:** Incentive ROI analytics, sales performance insights

---

## 3. Employee Gifting

Automated and customized gifting workflows for every use case.

- Automate onboarding gifts, birthdays, anniversaries — no milestone missed
- HRIS integration for automatic triggers
- Custom programs for all holidays with minimal manual effort
- Recognize personal milestones (weddings, new homes, graduations, etc.)
- **Swag Support:** Bulk orders for custom-branded merchandise with fully managed logistics
- **Year Books:** Digital or printed celebration books with team contributions

---

## 4. Surveys & Feedback

Run a continuous listening strategy with science-backed surveys and AI analytics.

- **Survey types:** eNPS, lifecycle, pulse, 360° feedback
- **Sentiment Analysis:** Actionable sentiment insights from responses
- **Customization:** Modify templates or create from scratch with an extensive question bank
- **Mood-o-Meter:** Track emotional tone of employees in real time
- **AI bot for surveys:** Real-time listening & insights *(Launch June 2026)*

---

## 5. Employee Benefits

Simplify benefits management while maximizing tax savings and flexibility.

- **Multi-benefit prepaid card:** Tax-saving on fuel, telecom, books, meals, etc.
- **Fringe benefits:** Flexible Lifestyle Spending Accounts (LSAs) for fitness, wellness, travel, upskilling, family care
- **Early salary access:** Financial wellness through pay-day loans for unplanned expenses
- **Device leasing:** Best-in-class devices for your workforce at zero running cost
- **Perks & discounts:** Best-in-class offers on travel, food, utilities, gadgets, services

---

## 6. Wellness & Fitness

- **Fitness Challenges:** Step, walking, running, cycling, and activity-based challenges
- **Wearables Integration:** Sync data from popular wellness platforms and devices
- **Gamification:** Leaderboards, badges, milestones, team competitions
- **Wellness Campaigns:** Themed programs like "10K Steps Challenge," "30-Day Fitness Challenge"
- **Rewards for Participation:** Points, gift cards, or rewards for completing challenges
- **Social Engagement:** Share progress, celebrate on the Empuls social feed
- **Doctor Consultations & Health Checkups:** Via wellness partners
- **Gym & Fitness Memberships:** Subsidized or discounted programs
- **Holistic Wellness:** Mental wellness, nutrition guidance, health coaching

---

## 7. Social Intranet

Foster transparency, connection, and community in hybrid workplaces.

- Personalized content feed with posts, videos, polls, and celebrations
- Rich text media editor with advanced styling options
- Wishboards and Yearbooks for milestones
- **Spotlight:** Pin org-wide announcements to feed
- "Ask Me Anything" formats, townhalls, organizational charts
- Community groups by location, interests, hobbies, projects
- **Employee directory:** Find peers and understand team structures
- **Home page editor:** Fully customizable homepage with quick links and widgets
- LinkedIn integration — share recognitions externally

---

## 8. AI Copilot (Em)

Em, the agentic AI in Empuls, is a proactive engagement assistant.

- **AI skill mapping:** Extract employee skills from recognition messages to build skill profiles
- **Recognition coach:** Identifies who hasn't been recognized recently
- **Award automation:** Auto-generates personalized awards with visuals
- **Predictive analytics:** Forecast attrition risk and program impact
- **AI-driven survey insights:** Auto-summarize responses and recommend actions
- **Content moderation:** Flag inappropriate posts to maintain workplace decorum
