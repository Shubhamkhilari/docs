# Empuls — AI-Powered Employee Engagement Platform

## What is Empuls?

Empuls is an AI-powered platform designed to help companies meaningfully engage employees across the entire lifecycle — from onboarding to exit. It caters to HR teams, managers, and leadership looking to boost engagement, build a culture of appreciation, streamline communication, and provide personalized employee experiences at scale.

**Product owners:** Mallikarjun & Chinmay
**Top industries:** IT/ITES/SaaS, BFSI, Health, FMCG
**Minimum ICP size:** 200+ employees

## The Big Picture

Empuls helps organizations build a workplace where employees are:

- **Recognized** for contributions
- **Rewarded** for performance
- **Motivated** through incentives
- **Celebrated** through milestones
- **Connected** through community
- **Heard** through feedback
- **Supported** through benefits

---

## Core Modules

1. **Rewards & Recognition (R&R)** — Peer, manager, milestone, and value-based recognition
2. **Sales Incentives & Commissions** — Automated incentive plans, real-time dashboards, gamification
3. **Employee Gifting** — Automated gifting workflows for every occasion
4. **Surveys & Feedback** — eNPS, lifecycle, pulse, 360° feedback with AI insights
5. **Employee Benefits** — Prepaid cards, fringe benefits, device leasing, early salary access
6. **Wellness & Fitness** — Challenges, fitness apps integration, gym memberships
7. **Social Intranet** — Personalized feed, communities, announcements, org chart
8. **AI Copilot (Em)** — Predictive analytics, skill mapping, award automation, content moderation
