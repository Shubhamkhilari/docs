# Empuls — GTM, Sales & ICP

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Min. employee count | 200+ employees |
| Top industries | IT/ITES/SaaS, BFSI, Health, FMCG |
| Best geographies | USA, India, UK/EU, then other regions |
| Typical budget | SaaS: $0–100K/yr + margin + expiry |

### Disqualifying Attributes
- Less than 50 employees
- No HR function
- No HRIS
- No employee engagement mandate

### Good Tech Stack Signals (Easy Wins)
- HRIS: Workday, SAP, Oracle, ADP, HiBob, UKG, BambooHR, Darwinbox, Keka
- Collaboration: Slack, MS Teams, Outlook, Viva, GSuite
- SSO enabled
- Clean, centralized employee data

---

## Buying Triggers

- HR tech refresh or tool consolidation
- Low engagement / low eNPS score
- Rapid hiring or scaling
- Remote or hybrid work transition
- Automation of gifting, rewards, sales incentives, surveys, or wellness initiatives

### "Ready to Buy" Signals
- Budget approved, executive sponsor identified (CHRO or Head of HR)
- Clear trigger: low eNPS, replacing a tool, or HR tech refresh
- Defined rollout timelines
- IT alignment for integration
- Agreed success metrics (adoption, engagement lift, retention impact)

---

## Economic Buyers (Decision Makers)

- CHRO / Chief People Officer
- Head of HR / HRBP
- Employee Experience / Culture Leaders
- Director of Total Rewards
- CXO in mid-market
- CRO / Chief Revenue Officer (for sales incentives)
- Director of Employee Wellness / Benefits

## Key Evaluators
CHRO, CXO, Culture, Rewards, Compensation & Benefits teams

## Influencers / Blockers
- **IT:** Security & integration approval
- **Finance:** Budget allocation, reward liability
- **Procurement:** Contract & vendor evaluation
- **Legal:** DPA, data residency, compliance review
- **CEO/MD** in startups

---

## Procurement & Legal Timeline

Triggered at NDA/MSA stage and includes:
- InfoSec/security assessments (SOC 2, GDPR, DPDA, VAPT reports)
- Commercial finalization: pricing, PO, vendor onboarding, payment terms

**Common requirements:** SOC2/ISO/GDPR docs, VAPT reports, MFA confirmation, Security questionnaires, DPDA & SLA

**Timeline:** 2–6 weeks for enterprise

---

## Top Decision Criteria

1. Security / compliance
2. Ease of integrations with existing systems
3. Pricing / value for money
4. Global support
5. Vendor reputation
6. Product features, quality, UX/UI
7. Rewards catalog breadth

## Typical Buyer Pain

- Low engagement, manual or biased recognition programs
- Poor survey insights
- Fragmented communication
- Difficulty managing global rewards catalog
- Multiple point solutions for surveys/R&R/gifting/communication
- Cost vs ROI concerns
