## Hiring Philosophy

Xoxoday looks for:
1. **Culture & values alignment** — long-term success comes from shared values and purpose
2. **Attitude over aptitude** — ownership and work ethic over purely technical skills
3. **Future potential over past credentials** — ability to grow, adapt, and deliver results
