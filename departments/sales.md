# Sales & Revenue

## Team Structure

The sales team is divided into three types:

| Team | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Hunting** | Acquiring net new clients |
| **Farming** | Managing existing clients for expansion and renewal |
| **Partnerships** | Managing integration partners, resellers, and ISVs |

Hunting & farming are further divided into **SMB** and **Enterprise** segments, with regional team members based in cities and countries where we do business.

---

## Supporting Functions

- **RevOps:** The brain behind the sales function. Manages sales data, reviews, insights across the funnel. Oversees revenue targets, risks, growth levers, and support to the sales team. Centrally located in Bangalore for all products and all geos.
- **SDR (Sales Development Representatives):** Generates meetings from inbound and outbound channels (primarily calling).
- **Marketing:** Provides MQLs, SQLs, and collaterals.

---

## SDR Sub-Teams

1. SDR for existing accounts (upsell/cross-sell)
2. SDR for partnership/reseller-led meetings
3. SDR for regional sales teams
4. SDR for SMB/enterprise accounts in hunting

SDRs are typically mapped to AEs for close collaboration and also work closely with the ABM team.

---

## Sales Process

```
New lead (inbound/outbound)
→ Enrichment from tools
→ Meeting setup via SDR
→ AE manages meetings/demos
→ Pipeline created
→ Deal closed or lost
→ Acquired client goes to farming team for account management
```

---

## CRM

**HubSpot** is the single source of truth for all sales data (hunting, farming, and partnerships).

---

## Sales Incentives

Sales team earns incentives as a **percentage of net revenue** from closed deals.

---

## Deal Desk

Used for large, complicated deals where:
- Client is large
- Implementation timeline is long and high effort
- Revenue is large
- Multiple stakeholders across teams are involved

Purpose: Ensure we don't commit to a wrong deal, wrong timeline, or wrong commercials.

---

## RFP Process

```
RFP received
→ Filled by RFP team (liaising with various stakeholders)
→ RFP submission
→ RFP won or lost
→ If won → Sales team for commercials
→ After payments/PO → Implementation/onboarding team
```

## Key Metrics to Know

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| ARR | Annual Recurring Revenue |
| MRR | Monthly Recurring Revenue |
| ACV | Annual Contract Value |
| TCV/LTV | Total Contract Value |
| GMV | Gross Merchandise Value |
| NRR | Net Revenue Retention |
| Pipeline | Total value of active opportunities |
| Win Rate | % of deals successfully closed |
| MQL | Marketing Qualified Lead |
| SQL | Sales Qualified Lead |
| Churn | Loss of customers or recurring revenue |
| Upsell | Higher-value offering to existing customer |
| Cross-sell | Additional product/geography/department to existing customer |
