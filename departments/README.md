## Client Lifecycle Flow

```
New Lead (Inbound/Outbound)
    ↓ Enrichment from tools
    ↓ Meeting setup via SDR
    ↓ AE manages demos
    ↓ Pipeline created
    ↓ Deal closed
    ↓ Client onboarding (Implementation team)
    ↓ Client goes live
    ↓ CSM team takes over (account management)
    ↓ Farming team works on expansion
    ↓ Renewal
```
