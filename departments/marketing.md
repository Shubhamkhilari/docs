# Marketing

## Overview

- Centrally located in Bangalore; USA has a senior person leading US marketing
- Works across all products — no product bifurcation
- Works closely with sales to generate meetings, pipeline, and provide sales collaterals

---

## Marketing Sub-Teams

### Demand Generation
Responsible for generating MQLs, SQLs, and sales pipeline. Manages marketing budgets, CAC, Cost per meeting.

| Channel | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Adwords & PPC** | Generate MQLs & SQLs through paid ads. Budgets reduced; now largely used for retargeting & competition campaigns |
| **SEO, GEO, AEO** | Generate MQLs & SQLs through organic search. SEO contributes well to Xoxoday's new meetings, pipeline, and revenue |
| **ABM (Account-Based Marketing)** | Generate MQLs & SQLs through outbound emails, LinkedIn, etc. Works closely with SDR |
| **SDR** | Generate meetings from inbound & outbound channels (primarily calling). Strong contributors to meetings, pipeline & revenue |

### Events
- Manage sponsored events, "Carrots & Stakes" events, delegate events
- Events help reconnect with enterprise clients, build market credibility, understand market dynamics, and generate new meetings
- **Carrots and Stakes** is Xoxoday's flagship event — hosted across the year in multiple countries and formats
- Largest spend in marketing overall

### Social Media & PR
- Manages Xoxoday's social media: Instagram, LinkedIn
- Manages PR across regions

### ORM (Online Reputation Management)
Manages online reputation on Gartner, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Google Reviews, etc.

### Product Marketing & Sales Enablement
- Manages websites, sales decks, sales collaterals, battle cards, RFP responses
- Handles any marketing requirement for sales and client-facing teams

### Customer Marketing
- Works with CSM and sales teams on existing client marketing
- Manages testimonials, case studies, customer events

### RFP Team
Manages filling RFPs from all regions for all products

### Regional GTM
- Manages GTM for countries where we do business
- Handles regional marketing nuances in UAE, SEA, USA, UK
- Ensures regional sales teams get full marketing support

### Memberships
Manages industry memberships: IMA, CII, Nasscom, IAMAI, FICCI, ASSOCHAM, etc.

## Marketing to Sales Handover Process

```
MQL
→ BANT Qualification
→ SQL (meeting)
→ Sales Demo
→ Proposal
→ Negotiation
→ Deal Closure
→ Handover to Delivery & Implementation team
→ Client go live
→ CSM & Sales Farming team
→ Upsell / cross-sell
```

## Key Marketing Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| CAC | Customer Acquisition Cost — total cost to acquire a new customer |
| CPL | Cost Per Lead |
| Conversion Rate | % of leads that become customers |
| Organic Traffic | Website visitors from unpaid search |
| MQL | Marketing Qualified Lead |
| SQL | Sales Qualified Lead |
| Attribution | Identifying which marketing activities drove conversions |
