## Sub-Teams

| Team | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **AP (Accounts Payable)** | Manages all payments, expenses, payrolls |
| **AR (Accounts Receivable)** | Manages all client invoices and collections |
| **Analytics** | Manages financial data: P&L, Balance Sheet, MIS, Cash flows, and related data points |

| **Audit** | Manages statutory audits |
| **Corporate Development** | Manages funds, investor relations, M&A, and diligence |

## Key Finance Terms

| Term | Description |
|---|---|
| AR (Accounts Receivable) | Money owed to the company by customers |
| AP (Accounts Payable) | Payments owed to vendors |
| MIS (Management Information System) | Performance reporting dashboards |
| AOP (Annual Operating Plan) | Yearly budgeting and operational planning |
| EBITDA | Profitability measure excluding non-operational costs |
| GMV (Gross Merchandise Value) | Total value of rewards/catalog items purchased through platform |
| GMV Margin | Revenue earned from margins on reward catalog transactions |
| Breakage/Expiry | Revenue generated when rewards are purchased but not redeemed |
| Net Revenue | Total revenue from SaaS subscriptions, GMV margin, implementation fees, and breakage |
| NRR (Net Revenue Retention) | Revenue retained from existing customers after expansion and churn |
| GRR (Gross Revenue Retention) | Revenue retained from customers excluding expansions |
| Burn Rate | Rate at which the company spends capital |
| Runway | Duration company can operate before needing additional funding |
| Payback Period | Time taken to recover CAC |
| LTV (Customer Lifetime Value) | Total revenue expected from a customer over their lifetime |
| LTV:CAC Ratio | Relationship between customer value and acquisition cost |
