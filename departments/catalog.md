# Catalog & Category

## Overview

- Centrally located in Bangalore with POCs in Dubai, Indonesia, Africa, and USA
- Supports all products — no product bifurcation
- Works with sales, product, and client-facing teams

---

## What the Team Manages

The team is responsible for managing the **content, catalog, and categories** for rewards, incentives, gifting, and loyalty.

| Track | Details |
|---|---|
| **Gift Cards** | Sourcing, vendor management, catalog for gift cards across brands and regions |
| **Offers & Cashbacks** | Discount deals, cashback programs, branded offers |
| **Travel, Experiences, Flights, Hotels, Miles, Lounges** | Travel category catalog management |
| **Swags, Merchandise & Long Service Awards** | Branded merchandise, bulk orders, physical awards |
| **Prepaid Cards, Cashouts, Tax Benefit Cards** | Financial reward instruments |
| **Benefits (Wellness, Fitness, Gym, Lending, Insurance, etc.)** | Lifestyle benefits catalog |
| **Analytics** | Margins, upsell, cross-sell, expiry tracking |

## Key Terms

| Term | Description |
|---|---|
| Catalog | Collection of rewards, gift cards, experiences, and merchandise available on the platform |
| Vendor | A partner providing rewards or services listed in the catalog |
| SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) | A unique identifier for a specific reward or product |
| Vendor Margin | Revenue earned from negotiated pricing differences with vendors |
| Procurement | Process of sourcing and onboarding vendors and rewards |
| Catalog Coverage | Breadth of reward options available across geographies |
| Redemption | Process of a user claiming or using a reward |
| Redemption Rate | Percentage of issued rewards that are redeemed |
| Fulfillment | Process of delivering rewards to end users |
| Voucher Issuance | Creation and distribution of digital reward codes |
| Delivery SLA | Time commitment for reward fulfillment |
| Closed Loop Gift Card | Redeemable at a specific brand only |
| Open Loop Gift Card | Usable across multiple merchants (prepaid) |
| Reward Wallet | Digital balance allocated to users for redeeming rewards |
| Float | Funds held temporarily before redemption |
| Liability Balance | Outstanding value of issued but unused rewards |
| Breakage/Expiry Revenue | Revenue from unused rewards |
