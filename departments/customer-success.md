# Customer Success & Customer Care

---

## Customer Success (CSM)

### Role
**Proactively** manage existing clients to drive retention, expansion, and satisfaction.

### What CSMs Do
- Conduct Monthly Business Reviews (MBR) and Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR)
- Create upsell and cross-sell opportunities for the sales team
- Manage account retention and churn metrics
- Work on strong SLAs defined and agreed with clients

### Structure
- Regional CSM team members based in countries where we do business
- No bifurcation by product or geography — works across all products
- Centralized but globally distributed

---

## Customer Care & Reward Operations

### Role
Handle all inbound customer support tickets and manage physical reward operations.

### What the Team Does
- Centrally located in Bangalore to serve all regions
- Works across time zones including weekends in rosters (~24×7 coverage)
- Common for all products — manages issues through central ticketing system
- Closes tickets within defined SLAs
- Tracks CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score) and FRT (First Response Time)

### Physical Reward Operations
The physical reward operations team manages:
- Printing and logistics of physical rewards
- Physical gift cards
- Long service awards
- Custom physical gifts from clients

---

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| FRT (First Response Time) | Time taken to respond to a customer query |
| TAT (Turnaround Time) | Time taken to resolve customer issues |
| CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score) | Customer satisfaction rating after interactions |
| NPS (Net Promoter Score) | Customer loyalty and recommendation metric |
| MBR (Monthly Business Review) | Monthly performance review with clients |
| QBR (Quarterly Business Review) | Quarterly strategic discussion with clients |
| Churn | Loss of customers or recurring revenue |
| NRR (Net Revenue Retention) | Revenue retained after expansion and churn |
