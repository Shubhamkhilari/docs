# Partner Lifecycle

The partnership journey typically follows 7 steps from identification to success.

---

## Step 1 — Partner Identification

Look for partners who have:
- Enterprise client relationships
- Complementary products or services
- A need for Xoxoday solutions in their offerings
- Global or regional reach
- Strong influence in their market or industry

---

## Step 2 — Partner Qualification

Key qualification questions:
- Do they have enterprise clients?
- Are they active in our target markets?
- Do they have technical integration capability?
- Do they influence purchase decisions?
- Why are they keen to work with us?

---

## Step 3 — Commercial Alignment

Define the operating model:
- Who owns the lead — Partner, Xoxoday, or Joint?
- Revenue share model and percentages
- Sales ownership and go-to-market approach
- Implementation ownership
- Support model

---

## Step 4 — Partner Enablement

Equip partners with everything they need:
- Product training
- Sales decks and collaterals
- Integration documentation
- Pricing and proposal templates

---

## Step 5 — Go-to-Market

Joint GTM activities may include:
- Webinars and joint events
- Co-marketing campaigns
- Partner marketplace listings
- Cross-introductions: helping partners introduce each other's clients (works well with strong relationships)

---

## Step 6 — Deal Execution

Typical deal flow with partners:

```
Partner lead
→ Joint discovery
→ Proposal
→ Client contract
→ Implementation
```

---

## Step 7 — Partner Success

Success is measured by:
- Revenue generated through the partnership
- Number of active clients brought in
- Partner pipeline contribution

Regular reviews and enablement sessions keep partnerships healthy and growing.
