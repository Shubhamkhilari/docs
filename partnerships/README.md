## Why Partnerships Matter

- Growth flywheel with low CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- Expand into new geographies faster
- Access new customer segments
- Integrate into existing enterprise ecosystems
- Scale sales through trusted advisors and resellers
- Build platform stickiness through integrations

---

## Commercial Models

Partnerships can use one or more of the following:

| Model | Description |
|---|---|
| Annual Membership Fees | E.g., SAP, Workday, Oracle integration marketplaces |
| Revenue Share | 5–20% of net revenue shared with partner |
| Monthly Fixed Payment | Especially for subject matter experts who refer clients |
