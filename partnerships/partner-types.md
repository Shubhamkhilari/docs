# Partner Types

Xoxoday works with three primary partner categories.

---

## 1. Reseller / Channel Partners

**What they do:**
- Sell Xoxoday products to their clients
- Manage local client relationships
- Handle first-level engagement in some cases
- Refer clients through their network and earn commission on deal closure

**Examples:** Regional SaaS resellers, distribution partners

---

## 2. Integration / Technology Partners

Technology partnerships make Xoxoday part of the enterprise tech stack. The goal is to make Xoxoday the **default reward infrastructure** inside partner platforms.

| Integration Type | Examples | Purpose | Product |
|---|---|---|---|
| HRIS | SAP, UKG, Darwinbox, Workday | Employee rewards, benefits, gifts | Empuls, Plum |
| Fintech | Visa, Temenos, Mastercard, NI | Offers, rewards, marketplace, loyalty | Loyalife, Plum |
| CRM & Martech | Salesforce, HubSpot, Moengage | Sales incentives, ABM funnel incentives, campaign rewards | Loyalife, Empuls, Plum |
| Surveys | Medallia, Qualtrics | Survey response incentives | Plum |
| Loyalty Platforms | Capillary, Achievers, Awardco, Antavo | Rewards fulfillment, redemption marketplace | Plum |
| Fintech Apps | Paytm, Revolut | Loyalty points, marketplace, rewards API | Plum |
| Customer Support | Zendesk, ServiceNow | Gifts/rewards for customers | Plum |
| Referral Platforms | ReferralCandy | Reward referees | Plum |
| Other | Gaming, Crypto, LMS, Community, Gig platforms | — | Plum |

---

## 3. Consulting & Advisory Partners

These partners influence large enterprise decisions and recommend Xoxoday as the rewards/loyalty/incentive engine.

**Types:**
- HR consulting firms: Aon, Kognoz, Acengage, Mercer
- Loyalty consulting firms: NI
- Digital transformation consultants: PwC, Deloitte, BCG
- System integrators: TCS, Infosys, HCL
- Subject matter experts: Industry veterans who are freelancers or retired professionals

**What they help with:**
- Employee engagement strategy
- Loyalty program design
- Sales incentive consulting
- Rewards management programs
- Client referrals and revenue generation
