# Values & Working Principles

## Our 6 Core Values

### 1. Human First
Every decision starts with people — customers, employees, and partners. We build technology that uplifts humans, not just business outcomes.

**What this means:** Prioritize people in every decision. Treat customers, employees, and partners with empathy and respect. Balance business outcomes with human impact.

**What it does NOT mean:** Avoiding tough decisions, compromising performance standards, letting emotions override logic, or being overly accommodating at the cost of business.

---

### 2. Customer is Our Compass
We build everything with the end-user in mind. We listen deeply, solve real problems, and obsess over creating meaningful value.

**What this means:** Deeply understand customer needs. Solve real problems, not just build features. Focus on long-term customer success. Make decisions based on customer value.

**What it does NOT mean:** Saying "yes" to every request. Ignoring scalability or product vision. Building custom solutions that don't scale. Short-term appeasement over long-term impact.

---

### 3. Think Big, Act Bold
We challenge limits with curiosity and a growth mindset. We think big, act bold, and constantly seek to unlock value for customers, partners, and ourselves.

**What this means:** Dream ambitiously and challenge limits. Take calculated risks and innovate. Move fast and experiment. Seek new ways to create value.

**What it does NOT mean:** Being reckless or impulsive. Ignoring data or feedback. Overpromising without execution discipline. Chasing ideas without prioritization.

---

### 4. Be Open. Stay Humble.
We speak with honesty, listen with empathy, and act with transparency. We stay curious, value feedback, and learn from wins, failures, and each other.

**What this means:** Communicate transparently. Listen actively and respect diverse opinions. Accept feedback and learn continuously. Acknowledge mistakes openly.

**What it does NOT mean:** Lack of confidence in decisions. Avoiding difficult conversations. Oversharing sensitive information. Being passive instead of assertive.

---

### 5. Craft with Care
We sweat the details. From code to content to conversations — we take pride in the quality of everything we create.

**What this means:** Focus on quality and attention to detail. Try to do it right the first time to avoid rework. Deliver thoughtful and polished outcomes. Continuously learn and improve.

**What it does NOT mean:** Perfectionism that slows progress. Overengineering simple solutions. Delaying delivery unnecessarily. Ignoring timelines in pursuit of perfection.

---

### 6. Own It, End to End
We take ownership — not just of tasks, but of outcomes. We act with intent, urgency, and accountability.

**What this means:** Take responsibility for outcomes, not just tasks. Show urgency and follow through. Proactively solve problems. Collaborate to ensure success.

**What it does NOT mean:** Working in isolation without alignment. Avoiding escalation when needed. Blaming others when things go wrong. Taking ownership without accountability.

---

## 10 Working Principles

### 1. Default to Ownership
- Don't wait to be told — act
- Bring possible answers along with problems
- Think in outcomes, not tasks
- "Not my job" doesn't exist here

### 2. Speed Over Perfection
- Ship fast, iterate faster
- Progress beats paralysis
- Learn through action
- A good solution today beats a perfect solution next month

### 3. Focus on Impact, Not Activity
- Measure success by outcomes
- Avoid busywork and unnecessary meetings
- Prioritize ruthlessly
- Effort doesn't equal impact

### 4. Say Things Early, Say Them Clearly
- Raise risks early
- Communicate openly
- Have clear agendas in any communication
- Silence is more dangerous than mistakes

### 5. Collaborate Before Escalate
- Solve together first
- Build trust across teams
- Avoid unnecessary hierarchy
- Try to resolve it directly before escalating

### 6. Think Like a Founder
- Treat resources like your own
- Balance growth with profitability
- Balance short term and long term
- Spend money and time as if it were yours

### 7. Data Guides, Judgment Decides
- Use data to inform decisions
- Combine analytics with experience
- Avoid decisions based only on opinions
- Neither gut nor data alone is enough

### 8. Global Mindset, Local Sensitivity
- Think beyond one market
- Respect cultural diversity
- Build scalable solutions
- What works in India may not work in the US or ME

### 9. Document So We Scale
- Write things down
- Share knowledge proactively
- Reduce dependency on individuals
- If it's not documented, it doesn't exist

### 10. Always Leave Things Better
- Improve processes continuously
- Fix what you touch
- Build for the future, not just today
- Don't pass forward broken systems
