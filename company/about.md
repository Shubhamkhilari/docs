# About Xoxoday

## Vision, Mission & Tagline

- **Vision:** To power the world's most rewarding relationships between businesses and the people they value
- **Mission:** To help organizations drive growth, trust, and performance by simplifying how they reward, engage, and build loyalty across customers, employees, and partners
- **Tagline:** Make Everyday Rewarding

"Make Everyday Rewarding" reflects our belief that motivation, loyalty, and engagement grow when recognition, incentives, benefits and rewards become part of everyday experiences across employees, customers, partners, and sales ecosystems.

---

## Why We Do What We Do

At Xoxoday, we believe that every human interaction — whether with a customer, an employee, a sales partner, or a channel — is an opportunity to create joy. We tap into the universal desire to feel valued and to grow. Even the smallest acts of reward or giving can drive motivation, deepen trust, and spark lasting connections.

At the heart of every organization are relationships built through everyday moments:
- An employee going the extra mile
- A salesperson closing a difficult deal
- A partner bringing new business
- A customer choosing your brand again

When these moments are recognized, appreciated, and rewarded, they create something powerful: motivation, loyalty, and lasting connection.

## Company History

### 2012 — The Beginning
Xoxoday started as **Giftxoxo** in a 1,000 sq. ft. room in Old Airport Road, Bangalore, with just one table and 5 people. Co-founders: Sumit Khandelwal, Manoj Agarwal, Kushal Agrawal, and Abhishek Kumar. Abhimanyu Choudhary joined as a co-founder in 2016.

### 2013 — Discovering Corporate Gifting
Introduced an umbrella voucher allowing recipients to choose their own rewards — a concept of flexibility that caught on fast. By year-end: 40+ clients including Cognizant, Wipro, and Pepsi.

### 2014 — The Big Break
Raised first angel investment from Kshatriya Ventures. **Diwali 2014** — received first major order worth ₹2 crores from Flipkart. Focus shifted to experience gifting.

### 2015 — Scaling the Experience
- First large-scale gift box order from HCL
- New clients: Siemens, HP, Applied Materials, Accenture, Infosys
- First international client: Achievers
- Team crossed **50+**

### 2016 — Building the Product Muscle
- Hired engineers from IITs and premier colleges
- Launched first SaaS product: **XoxoEngage** (employee awards & recognition)
- Strategic investment from **Mahindra Group**
- Client base grew to 400+ including Adobe, Bajaj Allianz, HDFC Bank
- Began exploring microservices architecture

### 2017 — A New Identity
- Rebranded from Giftxoxo to **Xoxoday**
- Launched **Plum** (rewards & incentives) and **Empuls** (employee engagement)
- Acquired BlueBulb & BookMyInterest (experience platform)
- Team: **100+**

### 2018 — Product, Brand & Global Thinking
- Named among **Top 50 Fastest Growing Tech Companies** by Deloitte (#9)
- Won Best HR Tech Platform award from People Matters
- Became finalists at SaaStr Europa
- Early entries into Dubai, USA, and Singapore

### 2019 — Filling Gaps in the Incentive Market
- Identified Sales & Channel Incentives as underserved; added new capabilities
- Hired full-time teams in USA & Dubai
- Team: **150+**

### 2020 — Growing Through Uncertainty
- Moved 100% remote during the pandemic
- Ranked **#20 in Deloitte Technology Fast 50**
- Digital marketing scaled — ~50 new SMB customers/month
- Tech stack matured, sales leadership strengthened

### 2021 — Recognition & Momentum
- TiE 50 Winner
- Winner of Unicorn Battle Asia
- **3rd in Global Unicorn Battle Finals**
- Top 15 Employee Recognition Platforms by SEMOSCLOUD
- Key Player in Channel Incentives by Forrester
- Team: **270+**

### 2022 — Funding, Acquisitions & Awards
- Secured **growth capital from Apis Partners PE**
- Acquired **Giift** → integrated and rebranded as **Loyalife** (loyalty management) and **Loopr** (offer management)
- Expanded into USA, Middle East, Southeast Asia
- Established formal board structure
- Awards: FICCI TNIE Best SaaS Startup, ASSOCHAM Fintech Award, BW Techtors Award, Salesforce Startup Program, Forbes DGEMS Select 200 Global
- Team: **400+**

### 2023–2025 — Still Building & Growing
Strengthening leadership, processes, product, partnerships, and global expansion. Today:
- 3,000+ customers across 50+ countries
- 10M+ reward options
- 65M+ end users (including banking customers)
- 100,000+ daily transactions
- $1B+ in annual GMV
- 1M+ merchants, 250+ integrations
